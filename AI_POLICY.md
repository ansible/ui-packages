# Policy for AI-assisted contributions

This repository follows the [Ansible Community Policy for AI-Assisted
Contributions](https://docs.ansible.com/projects/ansible-core/devel/community/ai_policy.html).

Contributors may use AI tools and remain responsible for their contributions.
Contributions must follow this repository's contributing guidelines, code of
conduct, and license requirements. When a significant part of a contribution
comes from AI output without substantial editing, disclose that use as
described in the Ansible policy (for example, with an `Assisted-by:` commit
trailer).
